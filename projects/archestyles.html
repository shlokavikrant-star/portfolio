ARCHESTYLES

Archestyles is an interactive styling tool that explores how people actually build outfits, inspired by everyday behaviors like saving looks, collecting references, and experimenting with combinations. It uses a drag-and-drop canvas to create outfits from your own wardrobe, functioning like a live moodboard. As you style, the system responds with editorial suggestions, encouraging decisions around proportion, layering, and overall direction. The platform also connects to curated visual references, allowing users to explore and expand specific aesthetics. Rather than generating final looks, Archestyles focuses on the process of styling how ideas evolve, how pieces interact, and how digital tools can support intuitive visual thinking.
