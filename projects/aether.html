Web Design Project

Aether

Aether is a creative direction generator that helps designers transform abstract ideas into complete visual systems. Instead of producing isolated assets, Aether builds structured brand directions including typography, color palettes, products, social strategies, positioning, and campaign concepts. The tool is designed for designers who build worlds rather than rely on templates.

This prototype demonstrates the concept of a creative direction generator. In the current version, outputs are pre-structured examples rather than dynamically generated results. The interface simulates how designers could explore complete brand systems from a single concept prompt.

Example prompts used in the prototype:

  • a banana themed makeup brand
  • a cinnamon roll haircare brand
  • a rocket inspired futuristic sneaker brand
  • a cotton candy skincare brand
  • a pizza scented luxury perfume brand
  • a volcano themed energy drink brand
  • a galaxy themed jewelry brand
