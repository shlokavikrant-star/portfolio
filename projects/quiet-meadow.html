Quiet Meadow

Quiet Meadow is an interactive reflective landscape where users plant thoughts as flowers that grow inside a living digital meadow.

The project explores emotional interaction design through atmosphere, subtle motion, and gentle rituals of reflection and release.

Users can plant thoughts, revisit them through a journal, and release them back into the environment, turning personal reflection into a living visual ecosystem.
