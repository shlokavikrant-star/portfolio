Reconstru(I)ctve

Medium: Photoshop

Reconstru(I)ctve explored how the self is continuously shaped by external influence, how our understanding of who we are shifts through environment, experience and the perception of others. The fragmented, blurred figure suggests an identity in motion rather than something fixed, reflecting how memory and self image evolve over time. By isolating the “I” within the title the piece draws attention to individuality while also questioning how much of the self is constructed internally versus rewritten though what surrounds us.
