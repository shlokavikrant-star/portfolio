Overcompensate

Medium: Photoshop

This artwork represents the feeling of creative paralysis the moment when the pursuit of perfection limits creation itself. The title, Overcompensate, refers to the tendency to overcorrect or overwork in search of a flawless outcome. The merging colours and overflowing forms depict multiple unfinished ideas colliding, expressing the chaos and pressure of artistic block. Subtle typographic elements, including the transform box reference, reflect constant editing, correction, and never feeling complete.
