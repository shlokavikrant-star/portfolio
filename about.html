About

Shloka Vikrant is a Mumbai-based communication designer whose work explores the intersection of fashion, visual identity, and editorial storytelling. Her practice moves across graphic design, digital experiences, and conceptual design, often drawing inspiration from fashion culture and contemporary visual narratives.

Through projects ranging from interactive web environments to editorial design studies, she approaches design as a way to build immersive visual worlds rather than isolated assets.

She is currently interested in creative direction, fashion communication, and the development of visual systems that combine storytelling, design, and cultural context.
