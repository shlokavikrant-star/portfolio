About

I am a Mumbai-based communication designer exploring the intersection of fashion, visual identity, and editorial storytelling. My work moves across graphic design, digital experiences, and conceptual projects, often drawing from fashion culture and contemporary visual narratives.

Through projects ranging from interactive web environments to editorial design studies, I approach design as a way to build immersive visual worlds rather than isolated assets.

I am particularly interested in creative direction, fashion communication, and developing visual systems that combine storytelling, design, and cultural context.

What I Bring

Audience Sensitivity

I design with a strong awareness of audience, shaping visuals that resonate clearly, even within niche or highly specific contexts.

Communication Through Simplicity

I focus on clarity through restrained color, form, and composition — creating work that communicates quickly and effectively without excess.

Visual Direction & Storytelling

I am interested in building cohesive visual worlds, from layout and typography to imagery and mood, ensuring every element contributes to a unified narrative.

Collaborative Creative Work

I enjoy working across roles within creative teams, contributing to editorial layouts, visual direction, and the development of a consistent look and feel across projects.
